# FALL! — Release Notes

## Version 0.1.0

**Release date:** September 4, 2026
**Platform:** PC

---

## About the game

**FALL!** is a fast, arcade-style falling game inspired by the classic Nokia game *Rapid Roll* — you roll down through the stage, dodging spikes and gaps, while a killer chase wall sinks behind you. Power-ups add a twist to every run.

This is the first playable build: a main menu, four hand-built levels, checkpoints, three working power-ups, and an end-of-version screen.

---

## How to play

- **Move:** roll left and right with the **arrow keys**. The ball moves with real momentum — it takes a moment to speed up, and doesn't stop on a dime.
- **Stay ahead of the chase wall:** it sinks from the top of the stage behind you. Fall into it and you're offscreened.
- **Watch out for spikes:** touching them kills you instantly.
- **Set your checkpoint:** roll into a checkpoint flag to mark your respawn point.
- **Dying:** you'll see a Game Over screen. Press any key to continue from your last checkpoint.
- **Quit:** press **Esc** any time to exit the game.

## What's new

### Power-ups (all last 5 seconds)

| Power-up | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Speed** | Rolls twice as fast — both acceleration and top speed. |
| **Slowfalling** | Softens your fall so you can glide down and thread tight gaps. |
| **Invincible** | Makes you immune to spikes, the chase wall, anything — for a few crucial seconds. |

> More power-ups (Fragile, Ice, Shooter, Slime, Time) are on the way.

### Levels

- **Level 1** — A gentle intro: platforms and checkpoints, no power-ups yet.
- **Level 2** — Your first **Speed** power-ups show up and the pace picks up.
- **Level 3** — **Speed** and **Slowfalling** mix, and the spikes get nastier.
- **Level 4** — The gauntlet: **Invincible** power-ups (use them wisely), more spikes, six checkpoints.
- Every level ends with a **Finish** banner — touch it to move on to the next stage.

### Menus & flow
- Start from the **main menu**.
- Touch the **Finish** banner at the end of each level to automatically load the next one.
- An **end-of-version** screen wraps up the content currently available.

---

## Known limitations

- No audio/music yet.
- No death animation yet — dying cuts straight back to your checkpoint.
- Only **Speed**, **Slowfalling**, and **Invincible** power-ups are playable; the rest are coming in a future update.

Enjoy the fall!
